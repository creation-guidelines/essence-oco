# Essence

**How to say why and how - of anything.**

A process that cannot say why it exists and how it gets there is carried by the memory of the people
in it. Essence is a way to write that down as four answers, each leading to the next, so that anyone
can read it, a tool can check it, and it can be drawn on one page.

## The four questions

### 1. Why does this exist? - the idea

Written for someone who knows nothing about it.

- Start where the reader stands: something they have lived, not your vocabulary.
- Name the problem in their words.
- Say the idea in one breath.
- Give one picture - an analogy, or a small story of one person.
- Admit what is still hard.
- Say where it stands today.

**The test:** after one reading, could a stranger tell someone else what this is and why it matters?

### 2. What must it reach? - the path

Steps on three horizons: **working** (it does its job), **perfection** (as good as it should be),
**ongoing perfection** (it stays so). Each step names its criteria - things that are measured, not
asserted.

**The test:** could two honest people disagree about whether a step is reached? Then its criteria
are not evidence yet.

### 3. How is each step delivered and proven? - the workings

For each step: what serves it - a tool, a check, a gatherer, a test, a practice - how, why we care,
and what evidence it leaves behind.

**The test:** remove the person who knows. Does the step still get delivered, and proven? A step that
nothing serves is a wish.

### 4. What is actually happening? - the work

The backlog, the log, the activity, the results - produced by the work itself wherever it can be.

**The test:** was this record made by the work, or written about it afterwards?

## Each leads to the next

Why leads to what; what to how; how to what happens. And back again: what happens should serve a
step, and a step should serve the idea.

**Whatever serves nothing is noise. Whatever nothing serves is a wish.** Written this way, both show.

## Honest by construction

- What has not been measured is not known - never failure.
- What is planned and not begun is not broken.
- A step is reached only when its evidence exists.

## It must capture itself

A method for saying why and how must be able to say its own. This repository tells its essence with
nothing but this method: its idea, its path and its workings are in [`data/`](data).

## Using it

The four answers are plain tables a project keeps in its own repository - `idea`, `milestones`,
`workings`, and its work - in the language of [ocoeco](https://github.com/open-commons-observatory/ocoeco)
(specification, section 11). `ocoeco init` gives a repository the place for them; `ocoeco check` reads
them as they will be read; the [Open Commons Observatory](https://open-commons-observatory.github.io/)
draws them, each project's on one page.
